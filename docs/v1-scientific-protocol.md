# v1 scientific protocol

The protocol is `configs/v1/protocol.json`, generated by `lob/v1/research/registry.py`. It is frozen in the v1 ledger
(`configs/v1/ledger.jsonl`) before any fresh v1 day is read. The referenced configuration files are hashed at the
freeze:
- `hypotheses-candidates.json`;
- `statistical-families.json`;
- `dataset-registry.json`;
- `consumed-inventory.json`.

`cleo v1 protocol verify` checks the protocol hash, those config hashes, the ledger hash chain with its replayed
rules, and every sealed design.

## Integrity rules (M2–M4, M8, M9)

- **Ledger.** It is append-only and SHA-256-chained. Every entry carries a reason, the git commit and a dirty flag.
  Guarded events refuse a dirty tree:
  - the freeze and amendments;
  - sealing a design;
  - opening or closing a namespace;
  - promotion;
  - any access in the confirm, replicate or vault namespaces.

  (In v0.9, 240 of 700 entries were dirty.)
- **Namespaces and roles.**

  | Namespace | Rule |
  |---|---|
  | `dev` | development and consumed data |
  | `explore` | explore pool plus consumed data |
  | `calibrate` | calibrate pool |
  | `confirm` | opened by a sealed design, after promotion |
  | `replicate` | opened after `confirm` is closed |
  | `vault` | opened once, after `confirm` and `replicate` are closed; never reopened |

  A guarded read must be listed by its sealed design.
- **Acquisition is not access.** Downloads are hashed into the ledger (`acquire`) without parsing, so data stay
  fresh until a ledgered `access`.
- **Seeds.** They come from SHA-256 of the namespace, design, study, purpose and index, and are disjoint across
  namespaces by construction.
- **Amendments.** They are numbered and hashed, and the previous document is archived. After any guarded access an
  amendment must be `posthoc`, and it is disclosed as an incident with both analyses.

## Statistics (M7, M130)

- **Independent unit:** the calendar date. The streams of one date are averaged into one cluster value.
- **Primary test:** a one-sided cluster-robust (CR1) mean test of H0 `mean ≤ threshold`, Student t on G − 1 degrees
  of freedom, Holm within the registered family at α = 0.05.
- **Secondary test:** an exact sign-flip test on the date clusters.
- **ESTABLISHED** requires both the primary Holm rejection and the sign-flip test at the same Holm-adjusted level.
- **At least 8 date clusters** per test, because 2^−8 < 0.00625 (the smallest Holm step of an 8-member family).
- **FAILED:** the upper 95 % cluster interval lies below the practical threshold.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** tested, but neither ESTABLISHED nor FAILED.
- **NOT_ESTABLISHED:** not promoted.
- **NOT_AVAILABLE / NOT_EVALUABLE:** the data, or a registered precondition, are missing.
- **Reported with every estimate:** per-stream heterogeneity and date × stream two-way intervals. They do not enter
  the decision.

## Promotion (M6, M7)

Promotion is frozen before any v1 pilot is read, and there is no split-half screen (v0.9 Amendment 3 is not carried).

- **Direct replications:** H1–H12, H45, H46 and H48. They are tested with a fixed 12 date clusters regardless of
  pilots, so the replication does not depend on pilot-driven selection.
- **Every other candidate** is promoted iff:
  1. its capabilities exist in the registered pools;
  2. a pilot exists on at least 6 explore date clusters;
  3. the planning effect (pilot cluster mean − 1 cluster SE) exceeds the threshold;
  4. cluster-t power is at least 0.8 with 8 ≤ G ≤ 40;
  5. compute is within the 60 h ceiling.
- **Confirmatory models** are fitted on the **calibrate** pool, never on the explore days that informed promotion.

## Hypotheses

H1–H48 are listed in `configs/v1/hypotheses-candidates.json`, each with:
- endpoint, unit value, test and practical threshold;
- streams, pools, fidelity, evidence-level target and required capability;
- its promotion path, and its v0.9 reference where it has one.

**Carried unchanged from v0.9, for exact replication:**
- the counterfactual assumption grid;
- the mandates and their notionals;
- conclusions C1–C3;
- the cost endpoints and the completion rules;
- the grid-normalisation rule and the data-quality thresholds.

v1 adds one quality check: an unreliable-message rate of at most 0.001.

## Realism gate (M132, M133)

- **Capability-specific:** a generator is accepted capability by capability.
- **Reference band:** the 5–95 % range of each metric over at least 8 calibrate-pool days, widened by the test day's
  bootstrap SE.
- **Classifier check:** real-vs-generated AUC ≤ 0.65 for ACCEPTABLE.
- **Fail-closed, never moved:** the thresholds are never moved, and a REJECTED capability never supports a claim
  that needs it.

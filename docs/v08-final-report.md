# v0.8 final report: decision-focused calibration, uncertainty and adaptive execution

**Branch and version.** Branch `research/v0.8-decision-uncertainty`, package version `0.8.0.dev0`.
**This is not a release:** there is no tag, no PyPI upload and no GitHub release.

**Protocol.** `configs/v08/protocol.json` (`b84e1e97…`), with two amendments:
1. Amendment 1 registered a secondary 400-lot mandate before any confirmatory run. Statuses are decided on the
   primary mandate only.
2. Amendment 2 is claim-registry bookkeeping, applied after every confirmatory run had sealed. It re-points
   CF-COVERAGE, STRESS and PORTFOLIO at the result keys the sealed studies actually write. It also splits out
   STRESS-SEARCH, PORTFOLIO-REAL and BASKET. No hypothesis, rule, threshold, design, seed or mandate changed.

The ledger is `configs/v08/consumption-ledger.jsonl` (412 entries); `cleo protocol-v08 verify` checks it, and the
pinned v0.7 history.

The narrative is in [v08-paper.md](v08-paper.md). Milestone coverage is in
[v08-requirement-coverage.md](v08-requirement-coverage.md). The machine-readable registry, hypothesis table, claim
graph and figures are regenerated by `cleo report-v08` (`results/v08/report/`). The public evidence bundle is
`examples/studies/v08` (41 sealed runs, verified with `cleo verify-v08 --bundle examples/studies/v08`).

**Evidence level.** Every confirmatory result below is **SYNTHETIC**: it comes from simulated worlds with known
ground truth, built from a posterior that the generator gate rejected (see below). None of it is evidence about
real markets. The fresh real holdouts could not be acquired, so every hypothesis that needed them is
NOT_AVAILABLE.

## Results by status

**ESTABLISHED (SYNTHETIC):** H1, H2, H3, H5, H9, H14, H19 and H21.
- Calibration that adapts to drift, to regimes or across generator classes predicted better than its frozen,
  global or single-model counterpart (H1–H3). The effects are small but clear: 0.017–0.061 in the registered
  predictive score.
- Epistemic uncertainty is material for some synthetic execution comparisons ([C08-H5] ESTABLISHED). For TWAP vs
  MPC it carries 55% of the variance; for TWAP vs POV, 62%.
- MPC and robust MPC lowered simulated CVaR cost vs TWAP ([C08-H9] ESTABLISHED, Holm over 3). The best
  development-tuned adaptive variant did not (+1.28 [−0.22, 4.60] bps, not rejected).
- Bounded counterfactual replays separated one registered pair, TWAP vs heuristic ([C08-H14] ESTABLISHED). The
  other four pairs stayed INCONCLUSIVE, and on the secondary 400-lot mandate no pair was determinate.
- Split-conformal intervals reached nominal coverage on exchangeable evaluation data ([C08-H19] ESTABLISHED). Under
  the registered shifts, coverage collapsed to 0.51–0.59 for every policy, and the intervals are only valid under
  exchangeability.
- Both H9 conclusions survived the sealed synthetic holdout ([C08-H21] ESTABLISHED). The holdout uses an unseen
  ecology/endogenous family, unseen regimes and unseen seeds. The CVaR improvements vs TWAP were mpc +9.44
  [4.94, 11.43] and mpc_robust +8.75 [4.10, 10.80] bps (12 worlds × 32 seeds, 0 INVALID episodes).

**FAILED (SYNTHETIC):** H11 and H12.
- OOD-aware abstention made selection worse ([C08-H11] FAILED). It fell back in 6.4% of cases, the incorrect-selection
  rate rose from 0.419 to 0.453, and mean regret rose from 211 to 1,499 bps.
- Safe fallback did not keep large degradations rare ([C08-H12] FAILED). It made them more frequent: 0.354 with
  fallback vs 0.319 without.

**NOT_ESTABLISHED (SYNTHETIC):** H4, H6, H7, H8, H10 and H18.
- Recovery: coverage is ≥ 0.83 for every parameter, but twelve recovery worlds cannot push the Wilson lower bound for
  the Hawkes decay above 0.60 ([C08-H4] NOT_ESTABLISHED).
- Calibration quality and decision regret are not associated in the registered direction ([C08-H6] NOT_ESTABLISHED).
- Two members were below 0.95 completion unconstrained, so both were considered. POV's constraint raised completion
  by 0.722 [0.473, 0.924] at +0.885 bps, within the 3 bps cost margin. The heuristic's constraint raised it by only
  0.106 [0.034, 0.209]: its lower bound misses the registered 0.05 ([C08-H7] NOT_ESTABLISHED).
- The CVaR and nominal criteria chose different parametric schedules, but the tail-regret difference is undetermined
  ([C08-H8] NOT_ESTABLISHED).
- Regime-aware selection did not beat one global policy ([C08-H10] NOT_ESTABLISHED).
- The post-drift gain is clear (0.086 [0.078, 0.096]), but the half-life of the gradual-rates scenario is not
  identifiable from its degradation curve ([C08-H18] NOT_ESTABLISHED).

**NOT_AVAILABLE (DATA_ACQUISITION_BLOCKED):** H13, H15, H16, H17 and H20.
- The registered acquisition rule probes an already-consumed control file before any holdout request. The probe
  returned HTTP 403 for the fresh-transfer days (2021-02-01; ledger entries 299–301) and for the final multi-axis
  holdout (2021-03-01; entries 406–408). No holdout byte was requested, and both holdouts stay fresh
  ([C08-DATA-BLOCKED]).
- Consumed data were never substituted.
- Retrospective rankings on consumed days are EXPLORATORY only (`transfer/ranking-retrospective`). Kendall τ ranged
  from −0.26 to 0.41 and pairwise accuracy from 0.33 to 0.74. Only BitMEX on the secondary mandate reached
  p = 0.032 against chance, before any multiplicity correction.

## Generator gate and model risk

Every candidate generator family was REJECTED by the registered posterior-predictive and discriminator gate, so the
gate result is NO_ACCEPTABLE_MODEL ([C08-GATE]). Discriminator AUC is 1.0 for each family, and support coverage is
0.0. The gate was not weakened. Two consequences follow:
- Every confirmatory study ran on plausible worlds drawn from the registered posterior anyway, as the protocol
  prescribes. Their results are statements about those synthetic worlds only.
- Posterior-predictive failure fractions by horizon are in [C08-PPC]. Local identifiability is weak (Hawkes
  condition number ≈ 231, effective rank 2; [C08-ID-V3]). The misspecification benchmark shows a Kendall τ of only
  0.14 between true and misspecified policy rankings ([C08-MISSPEC]).

## Exploratory and infrastructure results

These are EXPLORATORY or SYNTHETIC infrastructure results with no hypothesis attached:
- Real-chronology calibration half-life is not identifiable on the consumed days ([C08-HALF-LIFE-REAL]).
- Latency and stale-feed profiles changed simulated mean costs by at most 0.25 bps ([C08-LATENCY]). Cancel races
  were lost by MPC, robust MPC, adaptive TWAP, heuristic and random (5,624 in total), and by no other policy.
- Structured stress scenarios reorder the policies in simulation ([C08-STRESS]). Mean Kendall τ vs the unstressed
  ranking falls from 0.91 (queue disadvantage) to −0.01 (resilience collapse), with 0 INVALID episodes. A bounded,
  plausibility-constrained adversarial search found a plausible stress world ([C08-STRESS-SEARCH]).
- Synthetic routing ([C08-PORTFOLIO]), retrospective hypothetical routing on consumed days ([C08-PORTFOLIO-REAL]) and
  synthetic basket execution ([C08-BASKET]) are infrastructure demonstrations only.
- Synthetic per-path coverage of the counterfactual bounds ranges from 0.49 (POV) to 0.88 (MPC) on the primary
  mandate; these are SYNTHETIC bounded-replay checks ([C08-CF-COVERAGE]).
- Local research-workload timings are in [C08-BENCH]. They are not exchange or latency benchmarks. Serial and
  parallel runner results were identical.

## Engineering record

Every retained attempt is in the ledger with its directory, and none was deleted:
- **Aggregate-queue book defect.** Fractional simulated queue positions produced INVALID episodes in the first confirmatory
  wave, which was retained (entries 201–206). The defect was fixed in `3734c6b`, and the affected studies were
  rerun as `*-2` on the same sealed designs and seeds.
- **Fixed-share mixture defect.** `log(0)` when a zero-weight component dominated a bin; fixed in `0fa4a49`. It
  invalidated `explore/ood` and `confirm/abstention-3`, which were rerun as `explore/ood-2` and
  `confirm/abstention-4`.
- **Benchmark harness defect.** The write-once manifest broke repeated sealing timings; fixed in `9f899c4` (attempt
  `explore/benchmark`, rerun as `explore/benchmark-2`).
- **H21 ordering defect.** The H21 holdout reader opened its namespace before validating its inputs, and H20
  re-probed on every rerun; fixed in `c4e9ccc`. The attempt `results/v08/final` is retained, and the final analysis
  is `results/v08/final-2`. The holdout namespace was opened exactly once, and the failed attempt evaluated nothing.
- **Interrupted runs.** A host reboot interrupted `confirm/abstention-4`, `confirm/conformal` and `explore/ood-2`.
  Each resumed from its manifest-guarded checkpoints with the same sealed design and seeds, and each sealed with 0
  INVALID episodes.
- **Replay checks.** These were run on the sealed reruns: task-set manifests match, sampled episodes recompute
  byte-identically, and the analysis reproduces `result.json` exactly. Part of `confirm/conformal` was computed on
  `3734c6b` before the reboot. The only code change since then is unreachable from episode simulation, and the
  replay confirmed identical results.

| Stream | Source | Content | Used for |

Raw archives are downloaded once. Each is hashed while streaming, and Binance archives are checked against the
published checksum. The raw source hashes enter the ledger with the first access and may never change afterwards.
Raw data and day caches live under `data/v09/` and are gitignored. They are never committed or bundled. The data are
used for non-commercial research under the providers' terms.

## Day caches

Each registered day is reconstructed from its messages into a cache:
- 100 ms frames (counts by event class, touch prices, a 10-level depth state, traded volume, mid, validity);
- deep book snapshots (200 levels) inside the registered windows;
- all trades.

Execution studies use 24 windows per day, starting at HH:10:00 UTC. Each window is 300 s, with decisions every 5 s
and a 30 s warmup. Windows alternate buy and sell.

The quality engine checks:
- sequence gaps, duplicates, out-of-order messages and crossed books;
- the total duration of book gaps longer than 5 s (incident 1 corrected this);
- the valid-bin fraction and trade coverage.

A day that fails is QUALITY_REJECTED and is replaced only by a registered backup.

## Capabilities (what public feeds can and cannot identify)

- **Observable:**
  - incremental L2 depth by price level (Bybit);
  - L1 quotes (Binance);
  - trades with aggressor side;
  - exchange timestamps.
- **Never observable:**
  - order ids or individual orders;
  - queue positions (never observed);
  - hidden or iceberg orders;
  - trader identities or intent;
  - the reaction of other participants to a hypothetical order.
- **Consequences:**
  - fill-related quantities are back-of-queue clearance probabilities of hypothetical resting orders, or bounds;
  - executed volume beyond the displayed touch is reported as a proxy, never as identified hidden liquidity;
  - "toxicity" means a flow-risk proxy;
  - historical execution costs come from bounded counterfactual replay over a registered assumption grid (queue
    rule × latency × hidden fraction × impact). A direction counts as historically identified only when the bounds
    agree, and the costs are HYPOTHETICAL.
- **L1-only data (Binance):** these days identify only aggressive-only policies within the touch size, so only
  L1-identifiable conclusions (C6) can be tested across venues (H34).

Datasheets per pool and stream are regenerated by `cleo report-v09` (`datasheets.md` in the report run) from the
protocol and the ledger.

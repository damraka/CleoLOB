## Known limitations

- **Data.** Aggregate L2 and trades from two venues (Deribit; BitMEX on one fresh day only) in
  2020–2021. Exact FIFO, order identity, queue position, hidden liquidity and exact passive fills
  are NOT_AVAILABLE. Event resolution is 100 ms.
- **Posterior.** ABC at the reached tolerance, not the exact posterior; recovery is
  ASSUMPTION_DEPENDENT; the posterior is diffuse.
- **Realism.** Every family is separable from history (AUC ≈ 1) and almost no historical
  window lies in simulated support, so absolute realism claims are withheld and relative claims
  are the only ones tested.
- **Worlds.** 21 plausible worlds (16 posterior draws, 5 family point fits); the decomposition
  SDs are unstable at these counts.
- **Resolution.** The F8 MDE (0.924 bps) is close to the 1 bps margin; F12 (2.585 bps) is coarse.
- **Historical replay** has no market impact of the hypothetical parent and bounded fills only.
- **Learned policies** are PPO/DQN only (4 training seeds per variant); recurrent,
  distributional, constrained and offline RL are NOT_AVAILABLE.
- **No** live, alpha, profitability, production, HFT, exchange-latency or causal claim is made.

  hypothesis was established; scientific outcomes are in `docs/v07-final-report.md`.
- EXPLORATORY: run, but not preregistered or underpowered.
- ASSUMPTION_DEPENDENT: valid only under a stated, unverified assumption.
- NOT_AVAILABLE: impossible with the available data or hardware; the reason is given.

`m22/final-2`, `m16/transfer` → `m16/transfer-3`, `m15/execution` → `m15/execution-2`,
`m8/identifiability` → `identifiability-3`, `m6/posterior` → `posterior-2`. Earlier attempts
are retained.
